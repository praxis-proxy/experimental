This demo proves per app token budgets are enforced consistently across gateway replicas, and recover on their own as the window slides forward. Two independent Praxis AI gateways share one Valkey backed sliding window ledger, keyed by a per app header. App-a's first request, through Gateway A, is admitted. Its next request, through Gateway B, a separate process, is denied: both gateways consult the same shared ledger. Meanwhile app-b and app-c are each admitted on their own untouched budgets. Now watch the window itself. With a deliberately short ten second window, app-a's earlier reservation simply ages out on its own, and its next request is admitted again, with no manual reset. One shared ledger, isolated per app budgets, and automatic recovery, across a real process boundary.
