This demo proves per app token budgets are enforced consistently across gateway replicas, for two independently chosen rate limiting algorithms, and that each recovers on its own. Two independent Praxis AI gateways share one Valkey backed ledger. App-a and app-c are matched to the gold tier rule, a sliding window; app-b is matched to the silver tier rule, a token bucket. App-a's first request, through Gateway A, is admitted. Its next request, through Gateway B, a separate process, is denied: both gateways consult the same shared ledger. App-c, on the same sliding window rule, is admitted on its own untouched budget. Now app-b, on the token bucket rule, is admitted through Gateway A, and likewise denied through Gateway B: the shared ledger guarantee holds for token bucket too, not just sliding window. Now watch each budget recover on its own terms. App-a's window slides forward, its earlier reservation ages out, and it is admitted again, with no manual reset. App-b's bucket, meanwhile, refills continuously, token by token, and is also admitted again, typically sooner than a fixed window would allow. One shared ledger, two algorithms chosen per rule, isolated per app budgets, and automatic recovery, across a real process boundary.
